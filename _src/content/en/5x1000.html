What is the 5x1000?

The 5x1000 is a form of donation that allows Italian taxpayers to allocate five thousandths of their personal income tax (IRPEF) to support certain beneficiaries.

Who are the beneficiary organisations?

Beneficiaries can be:

  • Third-sector entities such as Politics Hub APS
  • Non-profit organisations (Onlus)
  • Scientific or health research institutions and universities
  • Municipalities (for social activities)
  • Bodies managing protected areas
  • Amateur sports associations

How do you allocate the 5x1000 to an organisation?

When completing your tax return, you need to enter the tax code of the chosen beneficiary organisation in the section dedicated to the 5x1000.

How much does it cost to allocate the 5x1000?

This operation involves no additional cost for the taxpayer, since the five thousandths are taken from tax already paid.

How are 5x1000 funds calculated for each organisation?

The Italian Revenue Agency collects the information provided by taxpayers and calculates the amounts allocated to each beneficiary based on the choices made. The funds are then distributed to the beneficiaries according to the instructions received.
