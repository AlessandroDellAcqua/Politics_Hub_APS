Il Poligono

The editorial project of Politics Hub.

In these first years of mass information through the internet, knowledge circulates more freely than ever, far from the restrictions of print or audiovisual media that once limited its reach. But unregulated access to any source seems to have produced the opposite effect of what was hoped for, increasing confusion rather than precise knowledge of the facts.

Now more than ever, then, we need a conscious stance that rejects the inertia of easy disinformation. We young people, born at the threshold of the twenty-first century and shortly after — almost digital natives — strongly feel the vocation not to be lazy consumers of a mechanism that no longer aims at truth, one in which convenient and partial truths spread ever wider, and in which opinions stray from the facts and even tend to alter them.

From these needs comes the editorial initiative "il Poligono", under the wing of the Politics Hub project. We want to provide examples of a journalistic ethic that, combined with a simple and clear treatment of content, can inspire a renewal of the approach to news — truly and sincerely critical.
