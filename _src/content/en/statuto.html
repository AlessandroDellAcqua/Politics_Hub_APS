Statute

Our "Constitution": rules and organisation are the foundations of the association's life; without them we cannot proceed.

This is a courtesy translation. The Italian version of the Statute is the only legally binding text. Read the Italian version.

Art. 1 - Name and registered office

In compliance with Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended, the Civil Code and applicable legislation, the Third Sector Entity named "POLITICS HUB A.P.S." is hereby established as an unrecognised, non-partisan and non-confessional association.

The association's logo is a light bulb with coloured internal connections and branches, with the words "POLITICS HUB" underneath.

The association has its registered office in the Municipality of Legnano, at the address recorded in the National Single Register of the Third Sector (R.U.N.T.S.). Transferring the registered office within the same Municipality shall be resolved by the members and shall not constitute a statutory amendment, but only an obligation to notify the competent offices.

Art. 2 - Statute

The social promotion association is governed by this Statute and acts in compliance with Legislative Decree no. 117 of 3 July 2017 as amended (hereinafter also the "Third Sector Code"), its implementing rules, applicable laws and the general principles of the legal system.

The Assembly may resolve to adopt one or more implementing regulations of the Statute to govern more specific organisational aspects, submitted for its approval by the Administrative Body.

Art. 3 - Effectiveness of the Statute

The Statute binds the members and the members of the association's bodies to its observance; it constitutes the fundamental rule of conduct for the association's activity.

Art. 4 - Interpretation of the Statute

The Statute is interpreted according to the rules on contracts and the criteria of Article 12 of the preliminary provisions to the Civil Code.

Art. 5 - Purposes and Activities

The association exclusively or primarily carries out one or more activities of general interest for the pursuit, on a non-profit basis, of civic, solidarity-based and socially useful purposes.

The activities it intends to carry out, mainly in favour of its members, their families or third parties, relying mainly on the voluntary work of its members, are:

  • organisation and management of cultural, artistic or recreational activities of social interest, including activities, also editorial, promoting and spreading the culture and practice of volunteering and of the activities of general interest referred to in this article;
  • promotion of the culture of legality, peace among peoples, non-violence and unarmed defence;
  • promotion and protection of human, civil, social and political rights, as well as the rights of consumers and users of the activities of general interest referred to in this article, promotion of equal opportunities and mutual aid initiatives, including time banks under Article 27 of Law no. 53 of 8 March 2000 and solidarity purchasing groups under Article 1, paragraph 266, of Law no. 244 of 24 December 2007.

By way of example and without limitation, these actions will take the form of:

  • promotion and organisation of cultural and political events, conferences and debates of general interest;
  • promotion, protection and support of human, civil, social and political rights, with particular attention to the world of youth;
  • editorial activity in print and on other media through the publication of proceedings, interviews and materials related to the association's purposes;
  • dissemination of cultural and political information through all means of communication;
  • promotion and organisation of partnerships and collaborations with public and private bodies, associations, businesses and companies, both national and international;
  • participation in cultural and political events promoted by other associations, public and private bodies.

The association may carry out its activities in collaboration with any other public or private institution, within the scope and limits of its statutory purposes.

The association may carry out activities directly connected to those listed above. Pursuant to Article 6 of the Third Sector Code, it may carry out activities other than those of general interest, secondary and instrumental to them, according to criteria and limits defined by ministerial decree: their identification is made by the Board of Directors.

The association may also carry out fundraising activities, respecting the principles of truthfulness, transparency and fairness towards supporters and the public, in accordance with Article 7 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

Art. 6 - Admission

Natural persons who share the association's aims and purposes and commit to carrying out the activities of general interest may become members.

Other third-sector or non-profit entities may join the association provided their number does not exceed fifty percent of the number of social promotion associations.

Membership applications submitted by minors must be countersigned by the person exercising parental responsibility over them.

The number of members is unlimited but may in no case be lower than the minimum required by law. If, after establishment, the number falls below the required minimum, the association must promptly notify the Office of the National Single Register and restore the number within one year.

Admission to the association is resolved by the Administrative Body upon application of the interested party, according to non-discriminatory criteria consistent with the purposes pursued and the activities of general interest. The resolution is communicated to the interested party and recorded in the members' register.

If the application is rejected, the Administrative Body communicates its decision to the interested party within 60 (sixty) days. The aspiring member may, within 60 (sixty) days of the rejection notice, request that the Board of Arbiters rule on the application.

Membership is for an indefinite period, without prejudice to the right of withdrawal. No category of temporary members is allowed. The membership fee is non-transferable, non-refundable and cannot be revalued.

Art. 7 - Categories of Members - Rights and duties

Members are divided into:

  • founding members: all those who are members on the date of the Assembly adopting this A.P.S. Statute;
  • ordinary members: those who, sharing the association's purposes, apply for admission to the Board of Directors, indicating their personal details and the address to which communications are to be sent, and declaring full knowledge and acceptance of these statutory rules and the resulting obligations, in particular regarding payment of membership fees.

Members have equal rights and duties. They have the right to:

  • elect the association's bodies and be elected to them;
  • be informed about the association's activities and monitor their progress;
  • take note of the agenda of the assemblies;
  • examine the association's books according to the rules established by law and by this Statute;
  • vote in the Assembly if registered in the members' register for at least three months and up to date with the payment of the membership fee;
  • report facts they deem censurable pursuant to Article 29 of the Third Sector Code.

They have the duty to:

  • respect this Statute and any internal regulations;
  • pay the membership fee in the amount, manner and terms established annually by the competent body.

Art. 8 - Volunteers and voluntary activity

The association may use volunteers in carrying out its activities and is required to record in a specific register the volunteers who carry out their activity on a non-occasional basis.

The volunteer member carries out their activity in favour of the community and the common good in a personal, spontaneous and free manner, without profit motives, even indirect ones, and exclusively for purposes of solidarity.

The status of volunteer member is incompatible with any form of subordinate or self-employed work relationship and with any other paid work relationship with the association.

The volunteer member's activity may not be remunerated in any way, not even by the beneficiary. Volunteer members may only be reimbursed for expenses actually incurred and documented for the activity performed, within the maximum limits and under the conditions previously established by the association. Flat-rate expense reimbursements are prohibited.

Volunteer members who carry out voluntary activities are insured against illness, injury, and for civil liability towards third parties pursuant to Article 18 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

Art. 9 - Loss of membership

Membership is lost by death, withdrawal or exclusion.

A member may withdraw from the association by written communication to the Administrative Body by registered letter or email; the withdrawal takes effect at the end of the financial year in which the communication was sent. The member remains in any case bound to the association for the commitments undertaken.

A member who seriously breaches the duties established by the Statute or the Regulations may be excluded from the association. Failure to pay the membership fee also constitutes grounds for exclusion.

Exclusion is resolved by the Administrative Body after hearing the justifications of the interested party. The exclusion resolution must be adequately communicated to the member, who may, within 60 (sixty) days of the communication, request that the Board of Arbiters rule on the matter.

Art. 10 - Bodies of the Association

The bodies of the association are:

  • the Members' Assembly;
  • the Board of Directors, also called the Administrative Body;
  • the President and possibly one or more Vice-Presidents;
  • the General Secretary;
  • the Treasurer;
  • the Supervisory Body, where required by law or where the Assembly resolves to appoint it voluntarily;
  • the Auditing Body, if required by law or where the Assembly resolves to appoint it voluntarily;
  • the Board of Arbiters.

Art. 11 - Assembly

The Assembly is composed of the association's members, registered in the Members' Register and up to date with payment of the membership fee. It is the sovereign body.

Each member is entitled to one vote and may be represented by another member by written proxy, including at the foot of the notice of call.

For underage members, the right to vote in the Assembly is exercised, until their eighteenth birthday, by the person exercising parental responsibility over them. Minors are excluded from standing for election.

Each member may represent up to a maximum of three members, or five if the number of members is no fewer than five hundred.

The Members' Assembly must be convened by the President of the Board of Directors at least twice a year: within 120 (one hundred and twenty) days of the close of the financial year, for the approval of the final accounts and, where applicable, the social report, and, when necessary, for the appointment of the members of the Board of Directors, the Supervisory Body, the Auditing Body and the Board of Arbiters. The Assembly is also convened in the 60 (sixty) days preceding the close of the financial year for the approval of the budget.

The Assembly must also be convened whenever the Board of Directors deems it necessary or when a reasoned request is made by at least one sixth of the members.

Assemblies are convened by notice indicating the day, time and place of the meeting and the list of matters to be discussed, sent to each member by letter, fax or email or by any means providing proof of receipt, at least eight days before the set date. The notice of call shall also set the date for the second call.

Directors do not have the right to vote in resolutions concerning their own liability.

Assembly meetings may also be held by telecommunication means, under the following conditions, to be recorded in the minutes:

  • the Chair of the meeting must be able to verify the identity of the participants, regulate the proceedings, and ascertain and announce the voting results;
  • the person taking the minutes must be able to adequately perceive the events of the meeting being recorded;
  • participants must be able to take part in the discussion and simultaneous voting on the items on the agenda, and to view, receive or transmit documents.

Where these conditions are met, the Assembly is deemed held in the place where the Chair and the minute-taker are present.

The Assembly is chaired by the President of the Board of Directors, in their absence by a Vice-President (if there are several Vice-Presidents, the eldest is chosen first) or, in the absence of a Vice-President, by a person designated by the participants, assisted by the General Secretary or, in their absence, by a Secretary appointed for the purpose.

Minutes of Assembly meetings are drawn up and signed by the Chair and the General Secretary or, in their absence, by a Secretary appointed for the purpose. Where required by law, or where the President deems it appropriate, the minutes are drawn up by a Notary designated for the purpose.

Assembly votes are always cast openly. Postal votes are not allowed. Electronic voting is allowed, provided the identity of the member participating and voting can be verified.

Art. 12 - Tasks of the Assembly

The Assembly:

  • determines the general programmatic lines of the association's activity;
  • approves the financial statements, the budget and the social report, when required;
  • appoints and removes the members of the association's bodies;
  • appoints and removes, when required, the person in charge of the statutory audit of the accounts;
  • resolves on the liability of the members of the association's bodies and brings liability actions against them;
  • resolves on amendments to the articles of incorporation or the Statute;
  • approves any Internal Regulations and resolves on any amendments to them;
  • approves any Regulations for Assembly proceedings (or electoral regulations);
  • approves the Regulations of the Board of Arbiters;
  • resolves on the dissolution, transformation, merger or demerger of the association;
  • resolves on the other matters assigned to its competence by law or by the Statute.

The Assembly may be ordinary or extraordinary. The extraordinary Assembly resolves on amendments to the Statute, on transformation, merger and demerger, and on dissolution and liquidation as well as the devolution of assets. The ordinary Assembly resolves on all other matters within the Assembly's competence.

Art. 13 - Ordinary Assembly

The ordinary Assembly is validly constituted on first call with the presence of half plus one of the members, present in person or by proxy, and on second call whatever the number of members present, in person or by proxy. The Assembly resolves by majority of the votes of those present.

Art. 14 - Extraordinary Assembly

The extraordinary Assembly amends the association's Statute and resolves on transformation, merger and demerger with the presence of at least 3/4 (three quarters) of the members and the favourable vote of the majority of the members. It resolves on dissolution, liquidation and the devolution of assets with the favourable vote of at least 3/4 (three quarters) of the members.

Art. 15 - Administrative Body, also called Board of Directors

The Administrative Body governs the association and operates in implementation of the will and general directions of the Assembly, to which it answers directly and by which it may be removed.

The association is governed and administered by a Board of Directors composed of a minimum of 3 (three) to a maximum of 13 (thirteen) members, including the President, appointed by the ordinary Members' Assembly; at least one third of them shall be chosen from among members who have belonged to the association for at least 2 (two) years.

Article 2382 of the Civil Code applies to the members of the Board. Article 2475-ter of the Civil Code applies to directors' conflicts of interest.

All adult members who do not belong to a political party may be elected to the Board of Directors. Membership of a political party by a Board member entails automatic forfeiture of office.

Unless otherwise resolved by the appointing Assembly, Board members hold office for 2 (two) financial years and may be re-elected. The Board's term expires on the date of the Assembly approving the final accounts for the financial year in which the mandate ends.

If one or more directors cease to hold office, the others replace them by appointing as director the first non-elected member candidate, applying the same replacement criterion until non-elected candidates are exhausted. In the absence of non-elected candidates, the Board of Directors convenes a Members' Assembly to elect new directors. Directors thus appointed or elected remain in office until the expiry of the Board of which they become part.

The office of director may be remunerated within the limits established by Article 8 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

The Board of Directors is convened at least forty-eight hours in advance, by letter, email or any means providing proof of receipt, indicating the date, time, place of the meeting and the items to be discussed. Board meetings and resolutions are valid, even without formal notice, when all serving directors attend.

Board meetings may also be held by telecommunication means, under the same conditions as for the Assembly, to be recorded in the minutes.

For Board resolutions to be valid, where convened, the presence of at least half of the directors is required and resolutions are passed by majority vote; in the event of a tie, the vote of the person chairing the meeting prevails.

Minutes of Board resolutions are drawn up and signed by the President and the General Secretary or by a person designated by the participants.

Directors are required to attend Board meetings. A director who fails to attend three consecutive meetings without justified reason or, if fewer, those held during an entire financial year, is considered to have resigned.

The Board meets whenever the President deems it necessary or when requested by a majority of the directors. Board meetings are chaired by the President or, in their absence, by a person designated by the participants.

The Board of Directors is vested with the powers of ordinary and extraordinary management of the association whose competence is not by law reserved exclusively to the Assembly, including, among others, the powers to:

  • administer the association;
  • convene the assemblies and implement their resolutions;
  • resolve on the admission of new members;
  • resolve on the exclusion of members;
  • draw up the budgets and final accounts and, where required, the social report, submit them to the Assembly for approval and attend to the further obligations required by law;
  • prepare all elements useful to the Assembly for the economic forecasting and planning of the financial year;
  • execute all deeds and contracts relating to the association's activities, purchase and sell movable property; determine the use of contributions, grants and financial means available to the association;
  • establish the amount of membership fees for each financial year;
  • submit to the Assembly, after appropriate examination, proposals, reports and motions formulated by the members and amendments to the Statute;
  • decide on the association's participation in calls, tenders or public selection procedures however named, preparing and signing the relevant documents;
  • entrust third parties with assignments for the achievement of the statutory purposes;
  • promote and/or organise events (seminars, study days, conferences, etc.) aimed at raising public awareness of the association's activity and purposes;
  • prepare any Regulations and any Ethical Charter of the association to be submitted to the Assembly for approval;
  • keep the association's books within its competence;
  • resolve on any matter not expressly reserved by this Statute to the Assembly or other bodies.

Art. 16 - Offices

The President of the association is the President of the Administrative Body and is appointed by the Assembly together with the other members of the Administrative Body. The Board of Directors may appoint one or more Vice-Presidents from among its members, and shall appoint the Treasurer and the General Secretary.

Art. 17 - President and Vice-Presidents

The President legally represents the association towards third parties and in court, holds office for the same term as the Board of Directors and may be reconfirmed one or more times.

The President chairs the Assembly, convenes and chairs the meetings of the Board of Directors, ensures the execution of its resolutions and normally acts as coordinator of the association's work.

The Vice-President(s), if appointed, replace(s) the President in the event of their impediment, in all functions and in representation towards third parties and in court. The Vice-President(s), if appointed, hold(s) office for the same term as the Board of Directors and may be re-elected.

The power of representation belongs to the President and the Vice-President(s) and is general; therefore, limitations of this power cannot be enforced against third parties unless recorded in the National Single Register of the Third Sector or unless it is proven that the third parties were aware of them.

Art. 18 - General Secretary and Treasurer

The General Secretary is elected by the Board of Directors from among its members. They remain in office for the same term as the Board and may be re-elected.

They are responsible for drafting the minutes of Board meetings, which they sign together with the President, except where the minutes are drawn up by a Notary and, if delegated by the Board, for coordinating and organising all the association's management and executive activity.

If they are unable to act, the Board may designate a Secretary to replace them, even temporarily, until the next Assembly.

The Treasurer is elected by the Board of Directors from among its members. They remain in office for the duration of the Board and may be re-elected.

The Treasurer controls and manages all the accounting activity carried out by the association. They manage the association's cash, carrying out payment and collection operations on the association's current accounts and keeping proper accounts, within the directives issued by the Board of Directors and the Assembly.

The Treasurer carries out the relevant checks, controls the keeping of the accounting books, prepares, from an accounting point of view, drafts of the final accounts and the budget, accompanying them with a suitable accounting report to be submitted to the Board of Directors, which will draw up the final version to be submitted to the Members' Assembly.

For tasks connected with treasury activity, they have legal representation of the association.

Art. 19 - Supervisory Body

The Supervisory Body is appointed in the cases and manners provided for by Article 30 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended. The Supervisory Body:

  • supervises compliance with the law and the Statute and respect for the principles of proper administration;
  • supervises the adequacy of the organisational, administrative and accounting structure and its actual functioning;
  • monitors compliance with civic, solidarity-based and socially useful purposes;
  • certifies that the social report, where required, has been drawn up in accordance with the association's guidelines. The social report records the results of the monitoring carried out.

It may, when the limits set by Article 31 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended are exceeded, carry out the statutory audit of the accounts: in that case it is composed of statutory auditors registered in the appropriate register. It may consist of a single member or of three standing members and two alternates.

The member(s) of the Supervisory Body may at any time carry out inspection and control activities and, to this end, may ask the directors for information on the progress of the association's operations or on specific matters.

Art. 20 - Statutory Auditing Body

Where required by law and where not entrusted to the Supervisory Body, the statutory audit is delegated to a statutory auditor or an auditing firm registered in the appropriate register.

Art. 21 - Board of Arbiters

The Board of Arbiters is composed of 3 (three) members, elected by the Assembly from among members not belonging to the Administrative Body. The person receiving the most votes becomes President of the Board of Arbiters.

The Board of Arbiters:

  • decides on appeals concerning the rejection of membership applications;
  • decides on appeals concerning the exclusion of a member.

The procedures for its convening and functioning are established by specific Regulations.

Art. 22 - Association books

The association is required to keep the following books:

  • the register of members and/or adherents when associations are among the members (Art. 32 para. 1), kept by the Administrative Body;
  • the book of meetings and resolutions of the assemblies, in which minutes drawn up by public deed must also be transcribed, kept by the Administrative Body;
  • the book of meetings and resolutions of the Administrative Body, the Supervisory Body and the Board of Arbiters, kept by the body to which they refer;
  • the register of volunteers, kept by the Administrative Body.

All members up to date with payment of the membership fee have the right to examine the association's books kept at the entity's registered office, within 20 (twenty) days of the request made to the competent body, at their own expense.

Art. 23 - Economic resources

The association's economic resources consist of:

  • membership fees;
  • public and private contributions;
  • donations and testamentary bequests;
  • property income;
  • fundraising activities;
  • reimbursements from agreements;
  • proceeds from the sale of goods and services to members and third parties, including through the performance of commercial, artisanal or agricultural economic activities, carried out in an auxiliary and subsidiary manner and in any case aimed at achieving the institutional objectives;
  • any other income permitted under Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

Art. 24 - Assets

The association's assets are movable property, registered movable property and immovable property. Immovable property and registered movable property may be purchased by the association and are registered in its name.

Immovable property, registered movable property and movable property located at the association's headquarters are listed in the inventory, which is kept at the association's headquarters and may be consulted by the members.

Art. 25 - Prohibition on distribution of profits and obligation to use the assets

The association is prohibited from distributing, even indirectly, profits and operating surpluses as well as funds, reserves or capital during its life pursuant to Article 8 paragraph 2 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended, and is obliged to use its assets, including any revenues, income, proceeds and receipts however named, for carrying out the statutory activity for the exclusive pursuit of the intended purposes.

Art. 26 - Financial statements

The association's financial year is annual and runs from 1 January to 31 (thirty-one) December of each year. The financial statements are drawn up pursuant to Articles 13 and 87 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended and the relevant implementing rules, and must truthfully and correctly represent the association's economic and financial situation.

The financial statements are prepared by the Administrative Body and approved by the ordinary Assembly within 120 (one hundred and twenty) days of the close of the financial year to which the final accounts refer, and filed with the National Single Register of the Third Sector by 30 June of each year.

Art. 27 - Social report

The social report is drawn up in the cases and manners provided for by Article 14 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

Art. 28 - Agreements

Agreements between the social promotion association and the public administrations referred to in Article 56 paragraph 1 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended are resolved by the Administrative Body, which also determines their implementation procedures, and are signed by the President of the association, as its legal representative, or by one of the Vice-Presidents.

A copy of each agreement is kept at the association's headquarters.

Art. 29 - Paid staff

The social promotion association may employ paid staff within the limits provided for by Article 36 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended. Relations between the association and paid staff are governed by law and by the Internal Regulations adopted by the association.

Art. 30 - Liability of the Association

For obligations undertaken by the persons representing the association, third parties may assert their rights over the common fund. The persons who acted in the name and on behalf of the association are personally and jointly liable for the obligations undertaken.

Art. 31 - Devolution of assets

In the event of extinction or dissolution, the remaining assets are devolved, according to the resolutions of the extraordinary Members' Assembly, subject to the positive opinion of the Office referred to in Article 45, paragraph 1 of the Third Sector Code and unless a different destination is required by law, to other Third Sector entities, pursuant to Article 9 of Legislative Decree 117/2017 as amended.

Art. 32 - Final provisions

For anything not provided for in this Statute, reference is made to the legislation in force and to the general principles of the legal system.
