Politics Hub APS

Politics Hub was born from the need to give value back to the idea of politics.

Our goal is to create spaces for dialogue among young people who want to engage critically with today's challenges, far from any party or electoral logic.

By meeting women and men who are protagonists locally and internationally, we explore political, economic and social issues.

About us

Politics Hub was founded at the end of 2019 in the city of Legnano (MI), initially as a simple group of friends, all between 17 and 25 years old.

In the first months of 2020, in order to carry out our initiatives more concretely, we established ourselves as an association.

We currently count more than 50 members, actively engaged in carrying out our projects, plus a considerable number of supporters.
