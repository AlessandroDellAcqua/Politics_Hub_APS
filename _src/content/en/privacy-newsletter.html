This notice is provided in compliance with Art. 13 of Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and Art. 13 of Legislative Decree no. 196/2003 (Personal Data Protection Code) and relates to all personal data processed in the manner indicated below.

The data controller

The Controller of the personal data collected is Politics Hub APS.

The personal data provided may be communicated to duly appointed recipients who will process the data as data processors and/or as authorised persons, in order to fulfil contracts or related purposes.

The complete list of data processors and authorised persons may be requested by writing to segreteria@politicshub.it.

Data transfer

If personal data is transferred outside the European Union for technical-operational purposes and to guarantee high continuity of service, the Controller ensures that the transfer is based on an adequacy decision of the Commission, so that the level of protection of natural persons guaranteed by current legislation, and in particular by EU Regulation 2016/679, is not compromised.

Data collected

The personal data processed is collected either because provided directly by the data subject or collected automatically.

Data provided directly by the data subject includes all personal data provided to the Controller in any way, directly by the data subject.

Automatically collected data is browsing data. Although not collected in order to be associated with the user's identity, such data could indirectly allow identification through processing and association with data collected by the Controller.

After a newsletter is sent, the platform used makes it possible to detect the opening of a message and the clicks made within the newsletter itself, together with details of the IP and browser/device used. The collection of this data is essential for the functioning of the implicit renewal systems of the processing (see Processing methods) and an integral part of the sending platform's operation.

Purposes of processing

The provision of personal data has as its legal basis contractual or pre-contractual requirements or the fulfilment of legal obligations to which the Controller is subject. For this reason, any refusal of processing or the failure to provide data, or inaccurate or partial provision, could make it impossible to properly provide the service or, for the Controller, to respond to the data subject's requests for information.

The provision of data for the purpose of sending newsletters for promotional, commercial, informational or research purposes is optional, and refusal to give consent will make it impossible to be updated about commercial initiatives and/or promotional campaigns, or to receive offers or other promotional material.

Withdrawal of consent

The data subject may withdraw their consent to receiving promotional and commercial communications immediately, by sending a request to segreteria@politicshub.it or by clicking on the unsubscribe link found in the footer of every promotional and commercial email received.

Recipients

The personal data provided may be communicated to duly appointed recipients who will process the data as data processors and/or authorised persons.

The Controller does not communicate any of the data subjects' information to third parties without their consent, except where required by law. Dissemination of the personal data processed is in any case excluded. The complete list of data processors, joint controllers and authorised persons may be requested by writing to segreteria@politicshub.it.

Processing methods

Personal data is processed with automated tools for the time strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, with in any case an annual review of the stored data in order to delete data deemed obsolete, unless the law requires archiving obligations.

Data processing normally takes place at the Controller's headquarters, by staff or external collaborators duly designated as data processors. The complete list of data processors and authorised persons may be requested by writing to segreteria@politicshub.it.

Specific security measures are observed to prevent data loss, unlawful or incorrect use, and unauthorised access.

The subscription and related processing are considered valid until the user unsubscribes (an option present in every email), or after 12 months from the last communication for which there is evidence of direct interaction (click, opening, reply).

Rights of the data subject

Pursuant to Articles 15–21 of EU Regulation 2016/679, each data subject is granted a series of rights.

  • Right of access: under Art. 15, the data subject has the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them is being processed and, where applicable, to obtain a copy. They also have the right to access the personal data concerning them and further information such as the purpose of the processing, the categories of recipients, the data retention period and the exercisable rights.
  • Right of rectification: under Art. 16, the data subject has the right to obtain the rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning them or its completion.
  • Right to erasure: the data subject has the right to obtain the erasure of personal data concerning them, without undue delay, where one of the grounds provided for by Art. 17 applies.
  • Right to restriction of processing: in the cases provided for by Art. 18 of Regulation 2016/679, the data subject has the right to obtain the restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability: the data subject has the right to receive, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, the personal data concerning them, and has the right to transmit such data to another controller without hindrance, as provided for by Art. 20 of Regulation 2016/679.
  • Right to object: the data subject has the right to object to the processing of personal data concerning them as provided for by Art. 21 of Regulation 2016/679.
  • The data subject also has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante della Privacy).

The above requests must be addressed in writing to the Data Controller. The Controller will provide a timely response to requests to exercise data subjects' rights within the time limits established by current legislation.

Any clarification or request for explanation may be addressed in writing to the Data Controller: we want to offer precise support both to the youngest and to the adults who interact with our newsletter.

Updates and changes

The Data Controller reserves the right to modify, supplement or periodically update this notice in compliance with applicable legislation or with measures adopted by the Data Protection Authority.

These changes or additions will be brought to the attention of the data subjects. We invite users to review the Privacy Policy regularly, to check the updated notice and decide whether or not to continue using the services offered.
