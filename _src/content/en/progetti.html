All Politics Hub projects.

Caffè Politico

Caffè Politico is a project through which Politics Hub promotes open and direct debate among young people on current affairs. Two elements characterise this initiative: the freedom granted to every participant to express their opinion, and the informality of the event, always accompanied by a coffee.

Go to Caffè Politico

Il Poligono

Il Poligono, the editorial project of Politics Hub. Providing examples of a journalistic ethic that can inspire a renewal of the approach to news — truly and sincerely critical. Twice a week, on the website and on our social channels.

Go to Il Poligono

Inside a Firm

INSIDE A FIRM was born from the need to bring young people closer to the world of work and business. In partnership with Confindustria Alto Milanese, Politics Hub created a series of video interviews with successful entrepreneurs to talk about work, the future and young people.

Go to Inside a Firm

Politics Talk

A young perspective on a world that has little time to listen to us: conversations with guests and bite-sized takes on economics, current affairs and society. It's not the usual talk show — this is POLITICS TALK, the Politics Hub podcast.

Go to Politics Talk
