Governing bodies

The association's bodies

As set out in the Statute, the association's bodies are the Members' Assembly, the Board of Directors, the President and Vice-Presidents, the General Secretary, the Treasurer and the Board of Arbiters.

Sovereign body

Members' Assembly

Sets the programmatic lines, approves the accounts and appoints the governing bodies.

Administrative body

Board of Directors

Governs the association and implements its general directions.

Guarantee

Board of Arbiters

Decides on appeals concerning the admission and exclusion of members.
