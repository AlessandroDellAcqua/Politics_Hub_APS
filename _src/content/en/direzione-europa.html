Direction Europe: initiatives to involve young Europeans in politics and society.

Politics Hub believes in Europe understood as a community of peoples united in the promotion of peace, ready to take on a central role on the international stage. With Direction Europe we want to be active promoters of the European integration process in its political, cultural and economic dimensions, as written in our Manifesto.

This page will collect the project's upcoming initiatives. To stay updated, subscribe to the newsletter from the Contacts page or follow us on social media.
