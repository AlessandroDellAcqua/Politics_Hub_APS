The Politics Hub Manifesto

Politics Hub is a non-partisan association with no electoral or profit aims. Born with the goal of proposing a critical approach to politics and today's challenges. By young people, for everyone. This is what we believe in.

1

We believe that the human being is the foundation, subject and purpose of social life

Politics Hub pays attention to the skills, attitudes and interests of all its members so that each one can feel represented and valued at their best in the activity that suits them most, for the benefit of the group as a whole. When meeting external personalities, the association's interest is directed to the person — specifically to their life experience and the ideals that move them — before their professional or institutional role. The association believes that politics must assess the needs present in society, seeking possible solutions and giving individuals the tools to contribute to its development.

2

A society that does not value its young people has no future

Politics Hub aims to bring young people closer to current socio-political issues, so that each of them develops a critical conscience about them. The association believes that politics must give credit to young people, and that this must be reciprocated by active participation in social and political life.

3

Knowledge is the key to conscious thinking and concrete action

Politics Hub believes in the value of education as the primary tool for the full realisation of the person, achievable only through broad and thorough training. Politics must invest in education and research, concretely guaranteeing access to study and the improvement of the educational offer, in relation to the needs of the world of work.

4

Competence and seriousness are the fundamental values for the development of the association and of society

Politics Hub promotes commitment and merit as pillars of a society that fosters the development of the individual as a common resource. "Commitment and merit" must be understood as alternative parameters to favouritism and indifference when assessing each person's suitability for their role in society. The association rewards quality, which is why it aims to create an environment that allows everyone to develop new skills and discover their passions.

5

Respect for the person, the foundation of the association, opens us to constructive dialogue with others

Politics Hub aims to foster a form of dialogue based on respect for others and for the plurality of opinions, the only path to a fruitful exchange of ideas. The association distances itself from a superficial and aggressive rhetoric that seeks easy consensus and the demolition of the adversary.

6

A democratic society cannot do without verified and unprejudiced information

Politics Hub believes in the social duty to spread free and in-depth information by building concrete spaces for dialogue between young people and experts. The association fights fake news through correct information, based on direct and verified sources, giving the public the clearest possible picture of what is being discussed.

7

Our being and our actions are unrenounceably democratic

Politics Hub believes in and is committed to promoting the democratic process understood as freedom of expression, political pluralism, and equality in both formal and substantive terms. Democracy is also understood as a clear alternative to authoritarianism and demagogy.

8

We condemn every form of human rights violation, as a denial of the dignity of the person

Politics Hub is actively committed to defending and promoting respect for human rights, as an essential element of democratic societies. These rights are understood in accordance with the European Convention on Human Rights, to which we refer.

9

Economic progress must also encompass the achievement of sustainable well-being

Politics Hub is in favour of a sustainable economic model that pursues the protection of the environment and the well-being of all members of the community. The association believes in corporate social responsibility, which must combine the pursuit of profit with collective benefit.

10

We recognise young people's right and duty to be an integral part of building the European project

Politics Hub believes in Europe understood as a community of peoples united in the promotion of peace, ready to take on a central role on the international stage. The association wants to be an active promoter of the European integration process in its political, cultural and economic dimensions.
