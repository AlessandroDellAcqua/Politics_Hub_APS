Where the Compass Points

A tool to find one's bearings in the world of economics, offering concrete help to the Red Cross.

Dove punta la bussola ("Where the Compass Points"): a compass to navigate the world of economics and the challenges of contemporary society, thanks to the contribution of six experts interviewed by Politics Hub. The book is in Italian.

Proceeds from sales are donated to the Red Cross committees of Legnano and Busto Arsizio, contributing to the support of the health system during the Covid-19 emergency.

This interview-book has two aims: on the one hand, to give even non-experts the means to find their way in the complex world of economics; on the other, to be a useful tool, for anyone who wishes, to navigate the challenges that this era of constant change poses to our society, interpreting aspects and phenomena of contemporary reality that touch us closely, sometimes without our noticing.

To this end, six experts contributed: Andrea Beltratti, Carlo Cottarelli, Francesco Daveri, Alberto Mingardi, Dolly Predovic, Federico Visconti, in dialogue with the young people of Politics Hub on topics within their expertise.

They offer many food-for-thought moments: from the analysis of our country's economic situation to a look at financial markets; from the future of businesses to the prospects taking shape for the new generations. References to the crisis generated by Covid-19 often recur within the interviews: it was impossible, after all, to remain indifferent to this epochal event.
