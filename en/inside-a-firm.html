Inside a Firm

In partnership with Confindustria Alto Milanese.

INSIDE A FIRM was born from the need to bring young people closer to the world of work and business. School career guidance is very limited in this respect, and too often students are disoriented when facing choices about their education and career.

In partnership with Confindustria Alto Milanese, Politics Hub created a series of video interviews with successful entrepreneurs to talk with them about work, the future and young people. The interviews are in Italian.

The interviews

The companies we met

Footwear — Parabiago, since 1953

Fratelli Rossetti

Fratelli Rossetti is an Italian company founded in 1953 and based in Parabiago. The founder, Renzo Rossetti, opened a shoemaker's shop in 1945 and in the early 1950s started the company with his brother, producing classic shoes. In 1979 the company logo was created and a few years later Renzo's three sons joined the company, including Diego, our interviewee.

Textiles — Robecchetto con Induno, since 1938

Candiani Denim

Candiani Denim is an Italian company founded in 1938 in Robecchetto con Induno, near Milan. Production takes place in two plants with almost 700 workers, and the company is the largest denim mill in Europe — and also the most environmentally sustainable.

Watch the interview
Hair Beauty — Rescaldina, since 1952

Lisap Laboratori Cosmetici S.p.A.

Lisap Laboratori Cosmetici S.p.A. is one of the most important Italian companies in the Hair Beauty sector, now in its third generation. Founded in Milan in 1952 thanks to the intuition of Carlo Locatelli, Lisap later moved its headquarters to Rescaldina, where it is still based.

Watch the interview
Optics — since 1985

Optec SpA

OPTEC SpA, founded in 1985, is an Italian company and a leader in the optics sector in Europe and worldwide. The standards and customisations of OPTEC products are the result of years of experience in imaging. Initially OPTEC supplied only optical lenses; today it offers integrated systems, optimising the performance of the whole system rather than of the single component. In January 2011 Optica Group was founded, the result of the collaboration agreement between OPTEC SpA and Tecnottica Consonni Srl.

Watch the interview
IT Consulting — since 1994

Reti S.p.A.

Established in 1994 from the visionary idea of Bruno Paneghini, Reti — a benefit company and the first Italian B Corp listed on Borsa Italiana's AIM market — is among the main Italian players in IT Consulting, specialising in System Integration services. It supports mid and large corporates in digital transformation with IT Solutions, Business Consulting and Managed Service Provider services built on the main Key Enabling Technologies (KET): Cyber Security, Big Data & Analytics and AI, IoT, Cloud.

Watch the interview
Advanced composites — since 1989

CBS

Since 1989 CBS has worked in the world of advanced composites. Thanks to the high level of know-how acquired over the years, CBS is now a reference company in the carbon fibre composites market for several sectors, with prestigious clients including Ferrari, Lamborghini and Leonardo. Its plant uses the most advanced production technologies, with machinery that allows it to follow every phase of the production process.

Watch the interview
Manufacturing — since 1961

Tacchificio Villa Cortese

Since 1961 Tacchificio Villa Cortese has combined, through a balance of craftsmanship and innovation, beauty with functionality. Their mission, expressed by the "Quality First" idea, is put into practice every day by pursuing perfection in the product thanks to production standards of absolute quality, oriented towards continuous improvement while keeping an eye on sustainability.
