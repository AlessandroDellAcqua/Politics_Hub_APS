Quiz Privacy Policy

Notice for participation in quizzes or initiatives promoted by Politics Hub APS.

This is a courtesy translation. The Italian version is the only legally binding text. Read the Italian version.

Dear User,

this Privacy Policy describes how Politics Hub APS collects, uses and shares the information collected from users (hereinafter "user") through its website and Google Forms for participation in quizzes or initiatives promoted by Politics Hub APS, pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (hereinafter "GDPR") on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data.

Data Controller

The Data Controller (hereinafter "controller") is Politics Hub APS, with registered office in Viale Gorizia 44, Legnano (MI), e-mail: info@politicshub.it and certified email (PEC) postacertificata@pec.politicshub.it.

The Data Processor is Mr. Matteo Monza, who can be reached at: segreteria@politicshub.it.

Purposes of processing and legal basis

The Controller processes users' personal data exclusively for the running of the initiative and in particular:

  1. to manage the initiative;
  2. to send communications related to the quiz, including updates, submission confirmations and related materials;
  3. to send communications (by email and/or newsletter) concerning activities promoted by the Controller of the same type as those previously used by the user, unless the user objects to the processing, which may be done at any time;
  4. to respond to users' requests and provide assistance;
  5. to comply with legal obligations.

The legal basis of the processing is the necessity of the processing for the management of the initiative the user has asked to join (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), the legitimate interest of the Controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and the legal obligations to which the Controller is subject (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Information collected

The following categories of personal information will be collected:

  1. contact information: first name, surname, email address;
  2. information related to the initiative: participation preferences, specific questions about the quiz.

Necessity of provision

The provision of the information indicated above is necessary as it is strictly linked to the management of the initiative. Refusal to provide it could make it impossible to register for and participate in the quiz organised by the Controller.

Communication of data and transfer abroad

Without prejudice to communications made in compliance with legal and contractual obligations, all data collected and processed may be communicated exclusively for the purposes specified above to the following categories of recipients:

  1. competent authorities and local bodies;
  2. supervisory and control authorities;
  3. providers of IT, telecommunications and network services;
  4. persons authorised to process the data;
  5. internal and external data processors.

Users' data may also come to the knowledge of partners, employees and collaborators appointed by the Controller as authorised persons and/or internal and external processors, who have been given specific instructions regarding the processing. In pursuing the purposes indicated above, the data may be communicated to other parties acting as independent controllers.

The data will be processed within the European Economic Area (EEA). Should it be transferred to recipients based outside the EU, in the absence of agreements ensuring an adequate level of protection of personal data, adequate protection measures will in any case be guaranteed, in full compliance with the requirements of applicable legislation on the transfer of personal data to third countries, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses provided by the European Commission.

Processing methods

Processing will take place in compliance with Art. 5 GDPR and the principles of lawfulness, fairness and transparency, adequacy and relevance, using paper and electronic means, by persons authorised by the Controller and with the adoption of adequate protection measures, so as to guarantee the security and confidentiality of the data. The personal data processed must also be accurate and, where necessary, updated, adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which it is processed. No automated decision-making will be carried out.

Data retention period

The data will be used by the Controller for the time strictly necessary to pursue the purposes for which it was collected and processed. In any case, including the retention period required by applicable legislation, the maximum data retention period is set at 10 years from the end of the relationship between the Controller and the user.

Retention for a longer period remains possible in relation to requests from public authorities and in relation to needs connected with the exercise of the Controller's right of defence in the event of disputes.

Rights of the user

Pursuant to Art. 15 et seq. of European Regulation 679/2016 (GDPR) and national legislation, the user may, in the manner and within the limits established by current legislation, exercise the following rights:

  1. request confirmation of the existence of personal data concerning them (right of access);
  2. know its origin;
  3. receive intelligible communication of it;
  4. obtain information about the logic, methods and purposes of the processing;
  5. request its updating, rectification, integration, deletion, transformation into anonymous form, or the blocking of data processed in breach of the law, including data that is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected;
  6. exercise the right to restriction and/or objection to the processing of data concerning them;
  7. exercise the right of withdrawal and data portability;
  8. lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority in Rome, pursuant to Art. 77 of European Regulation 679/2016, or apply to the judicial authorities.

The above rights may be exercised by written communication to the contacts indicated above. Before providing a response, the Controller may need to identify the user. A reply will be provided within 30 days of receipt of the request.
