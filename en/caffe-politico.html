Caffè Politico

Spaces of dialogue and sharing among young people, in an informal setting.

Caffè Politico is an initiative born with the goal of creating spaces for dialogue and sharing among young people — one of the main reasons Politics Hub itself was created.

The association is committed to offering the new generations the opportunity, often absent elsewhere, to express themselves in an informal context, where a true moment of authentic democracy and of genuine, enriching exchange is encouraged. The caffè politici, organised in Legnano and Milan, stand out for their fresh and innovative format.
