CV Privacy Policy

Notice for submitting a curriculum vitae.

This is a courtesy translation. The Italian version is the only legally binding text. Read the Italian version.

This Privacy Policy is intended to inform users about the processing of personal data collected following the submission of a curriculum vitae shared spontaneously by the data subject, in compliance with Art. 13 of Legislative Decree 196/03 - Personal Data Protection Code and with European Regulation 679/2016 ("GDPR").

The notice is addressed to those who connect to the Politics Hub APS website and intend to share their personal data by filling in one of the forms on the site or uploading a document.

The site www.politicshub.it is owned by Politics Hub APS, which guarantees compliance with the legislation on the protection of personal data (Legislative Decree 196/03 and European Regulation 679/2016).

Users should carefully read this Privacy Policy before filling in any electronic form on the site.

Data Controller

The Data Controller (hereinafter "controller") is Politics Hub APS, with registered office in Viale Gorizia 44, Legnano (MI), and can be contacted at: info@politicshub.it and postacertificata@pec.politicshub.it.

The Data Processor is Mr. Matteo Monza, who can be reached at: segreteria@politicshub.it.

Purposes of processing

The personal data shared will be processed by us solely in order to:

  1. facilitate the candidate's integration into the association according to their skills and passions;
  2. assess the commitment and seriousness shown by the candidate in completing the application.

Information collected

The following categories of personal information will be collected:

  1. contact information: first name, surname, email address, telephone number;
  2. information deriving from the curriculum vitae, if shared, or from written texts.

Processing methods

Processing will take place in compliance with Art. 5 GDPR and the principles of lawfulness, fairness and transparency, adequacy and relevance, using paper and electronic means, by persons authorised by the Controller and with the adoption of adequate protection measures, so as to guarantee the security and confidentiality of the data. The personal data processed must also be accurate and, where necessary, updated, adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which it is processed. No automated decision-making will be carried out.

Data retention period

In accordance with the GDPR, personal data will be kept for a limited time, until the completion of the candidate's path towards joining the association, or for a maximum period of one year.

Rights of data subjects

Those who have spontaneously shared their personal data may, in the manner and within the limits established by legislation, exercise the following rights:

  1. request access to their personal data and its rectification, updating or deletion;
  2. request confirmation of the existence of the personal data shared;
  3. exercise the right to withdraw the data.

The above rights may be exercised by written communication to the contacts indicated above. Before providing a response, the Controller may need to identify the user. A reply will be provided within 30 days of receipt of the request.

Please remember that the communication of personal data is optional and spontaneous. However, failure to complete the required fields may prevent a complete assessment of the application.
